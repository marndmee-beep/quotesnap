Short, practical notes on quoting, invoicing and getting paid without chasing people.

Writing a quote

  • List the work, don't summarise it. Itemised quotes get approved more often because the customer can see the value.
  • State your assumptions. "Assumes tiles are supplied by the customer" prevents arguments later.
  • Add an expiry. "Prices held for 14 days" nudges a decision without pressure.
  • Make the total easy to find. People look for one number first.

Invoicing

  • Invoice the day you finish. The longer you wait, the longer it sits unpaid.
  • Number every invoice. It makes chasing a late payment much easier.
  • Put the payment terms on the invoice itself, not only in the contract.

Getting paid on time

  • Agree the deposit up front. A deposit turns a maybe into a booking.
  • Send a polite reminder a day or two after the due date, not weeks later.
  • Keep a deposit non-refundable in writing for jobs that block out your calendar.
