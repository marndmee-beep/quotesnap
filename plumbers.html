Why tradespeople use it

  • No signup. You don't create an account or hand over an email address.
  • Works in the van. Open the link on your phone, quote the job before you've left the drive.
  • Labour and materials lines. Add a line per item with your own quantity and price.
  • Your terms. Put your payment terms, warranty note or deposit request at the bottom.
  • It remembers. Your business name and details stay saved for the next job.

How to write a quote that gets approved

  • Break the job into clear items rather than one lump sum, so the customer sees what they're paying for.
  • Say what's included and what isn't, such as waste removal or making good.
  • Put a date on it and say how long the price holds.
  • Ask for a deposit in the notes if the job is large.
