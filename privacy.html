Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

What we collect

QuoteSnap runs in your web browser. The business details, client details and line items you type are saved only in your own browser (local storage) so they are still there next time you visit. They are not sent to our servers, and we cannot see them. You can erase them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Payments

If you buy the watermark-removal upgrade, payment is handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. Stripe shares with us basic purchase details such as your email address and the amount paid. See Stripe's privacy policy for how it handles your data.

Hosting and analytics

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses as part of normal web hosting. We do not run advertising trackers.

Children

QuoteSnap is intended for business use and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes
