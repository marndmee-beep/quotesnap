Does a template cost anything?

No. Every template here opens the same free generator. You can create and download as many documents as you like, with no account and no limit.

What is on the free version?

One small line at the foot of the PDF reading "Made with QuoteSnap". A one-time $9 removes it from every document you make, with a 7-day money-back guarantee.

Can I add my own logo?

Not yet. Branding, saved client lists and recurring documents are on the roadmap for the next version.

Which currencies and taxes are supported?

Six currency symbols and any tax rate you set, including VAT or sales tax. Enter the rate as a percentage and it is calculated for you. You can also add a discount, and record a deposit already paid so the balance due is shown clearly.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It is a web page, so it works in any mobile browser with nothing to install.
